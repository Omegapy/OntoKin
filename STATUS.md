OntoKin’s [GOAL](GOAL.md) is an AI-agent language for editable meaningful 3D objects, animation and simulation through authoritative `.ok` source. Finite increments do not complete that mission.

The explicit bindings remain source `0.13` / `textured-surfaces-0.1`, immutable bundle/files `0.2`, Agent/0.13 and recorded GLB/0.13. One planar map per selected existing material region uses source coordinates before rigid placement. Content-pinned opaque PNGs retain their exact embedded bytes. Nearest sampling, clamp-to-edge, top-left UV origin and sRGB-to-linear multiplication remain fixed. At most four 256×256 images are admitted: 300,000 encoded bytes each, 1,200,000 aggregate, with separate decoded ceilings. PNG decoding, mapping and closure consume current execution authority. Appearance adds no body, mass, collision or motion authority. Transparency, automatic unwrapping, normal maps and deformation-aware mapping remain outside this increment.
