## F. Direct-tool comparison preparation

The GOAL's authoring-benefit requirement remains unmeasured. Before claiming improvement, publish a direct-tool baseline for the same complete bench and segmented-character tasks, the same held-out parameter variants and the same local repair. Identify the actual tool/version, allowed reusable preparation, operator/agent context, cost accounting and quality/physics/destination criteria.
