## Modeling direction

The recommended next finite increment is **polygon sections with explicitly authored three-dimensional rigid frames**. C077 sections share the local XY orientation and differ by Z station and authored XY coordinates. Giving each section its own explicit pose would allow a reusable ring loft to turn through space, supporting curved duct and handle forms while retaining source-edited rings, matched identities, regional materials and rigid recorded carrier motion.

The surface should remain an explicitly defined finite triangle mesh. A collection of authored oriented sections does not establish a continuous path, automatic sweep, interpolated frame field, smooth surface, bending behavior or physical tube. Source agents must author each frame and its correspondence. Reusable typed functions and generated parts can express repeated patterns without hidden geometric decisions.
