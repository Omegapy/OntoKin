# C194 public paired edit and reuse

This is a public exercise in a shared execution environment. Both authors have
the same task information, inspection access, finite allowances and output
requirements. Files and evaluator are not hidden. This protocol becomes active
only after the separately recorded C194 preparation qualifies both new targets
and the coordinator seals this protocol and its inputs before dispatch.

## Shared design and first edit

Start from the matching seven-part two-carriage gauge with its left stop near
face at 380 mm. Both routes use the same adopted `c193-experimental-0.1` source
binding, `blender-bullet-c193-0.1` backend, native constructor and recorded
`glb-guided-contact-0.1` mapping. Direct Python supplies literal design data
without parsing OntoKin source. The common native constructor and editable
starters are disclosed coordinator preparation, separate from author work.

Move only the left stop near face to **365 mm**. Preserve its dimensions,
identity and collider relationship. Preserve the other six parts, colors,
body and collider identities, following, drives, mass and physics settings.
The left shoe collider remains enabled. Expected left travel is 185 mm and
ideal first contact is 1.480 seconds. The right carriage must travel 300 mm
and retain 15 mm final clearance.

Submit your own editable source, actual native scene, raw and final GLBs,
native records, build receipts and a manifest identifying those bytes. Begin
with your provided 380 mm starter and record the source edit. Do not substitute
the coordinator's already prepared target source or artifacts for your work.
Preserve every failed command and invalid attempt. Stop after submitting the
first edit; the coordinator must independently accept it before author reuse.

## Later reuse

After acceptance, the same author starts from its own submitted source and
creates the **375 mm** stop variant. Expected left travel is 195 mm and ideal
contact is 1.560 seconds. Keep the first accepted source unchanged. Regenerate
both the first and reuse variants in fresh processes, demonstrating exact
canonical data, native motion and raw/final GLB equality for the first result.
Saved Blender file byte identity is not required. Retained literal variants
establish literal-source reuse; they do not establish general classes,
parameters, libraries or programmatic composition in this finite prototype.

## Equal finite allowances

- Exactly two fresh default authors with `fork_turns="none"`, no model or
  reasoning overrides, separate writable worktrees and branches, one per route.
  Authors do not delegate additional work during their measured phases.
- Each phase allows 20 minutes of wall time, at most three formal submissions,
  and at most six native build attempts including rehearsals and failures.
  Inspection and independent checker calls share that wall allowance. Stop at
  the first exhausted limit and submit the reached state, including failure.
- Each build retains 1,000,000 operations and 80,000 ms, using the unchanged
  common wrapper with its 100-second watchdog. Modeled motion lasts two seconds;
  that is separate from host computation time.
- Submitted bytes remain immutable. A requested repair uses a separately
  retained source and formal submission within the same phase allowance.
  Timed-stop substitution, shared-runtime changes, solver changes, sample
  retiming, tolerance changes, gate edits, trajectory editing and reconstruction
  of submitted scenes are prohibited.
- Both routes may use the same public artifact checker, scene inspector and
  receiving tools. Coordinator source rebuilds, replay, receiving and visual
  review are independent validation and are accounted separately.
- Provider token totals, cost, exact model snapshot and hidden internal turns
  are unavailable. No token cutoff or equal measured token use is claimed.

## Frozen acceptance

All adopted C193 source, geometry, contact and numerical gates remain unchanged.
Initial geometry tolerance is 0.2 mm; fixed-part or attachment drift 0.1 mm;
final travel and signed gap 0.5 mm; contact onset within 0.005 seconds of ideal;
feed and settled speed within 0.5 mm/s; settling begins 25 ms after ideal
contact; rebound 0.1 mm; penetration 0.5 mm. Native-sample, raw-sample and
continuous raw-to-final mapping errors must each be at most **0.2 mm**.
Preserve all 481 common 240 Hz times and every native carriage X sample.
All twenty unrelated pairs must remain separated through recorded intervals.

Native configuration retains thirteen objects, seven visible solids, two 1 kg
mass owners, one solver authority per lane and ten substeps per frame. World
and MOTOR solver settings remain 100 iterations; both straight-guide overrides
remain 400. Independently reopen and replay actual submitted scenes without
reconstruction or repair, comparing all 481 decoded records exactly. Source,
implementation, backend, manifest and accepted-store provenance must agree.

Check each actual delivery, even if both routes match. Clean coordinator
rebuilds must reproduce canonical data, native traces and raw/final GLBs.
Cross-route equality uses those same four files. Actual raw/final GLBs require
Khronos and Three.js readback; final GLBs require Blender import, measured
playback and visual inspection. Receiver success does not override a failed
native, source or numerical gate.

## Accounting and interpretation

Record phase start and submission times, every build and failed command,
source changes, first-pass acceptance, formal repair turns and coordinator
interventions. Retain first submissions before any repair. Preserve first
source/artifact identities across reuse. Report available command operation
costs separately from unavailable provider telemetry. No human intervention is
planned; record one if it occurs.

Preparation qualification is setup, not participant performance. A single
successful pair establishes this workflow on these targets. It does not show
general authoring advantage, cost superiority, statistical reliability, blind
evaluation, general contact, live destination physics or a production backend.
The inherited legacy driver and supplemental tail remain FAILED with 4,239
distinct passing methods and thirteen torque-read methods NOT RUN. This
unchanged cohort is not rerun or relabeled by the comparison.
