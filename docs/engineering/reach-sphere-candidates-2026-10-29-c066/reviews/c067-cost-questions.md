# Possible C067 lower-witness screen: independent cost questions

This is a proposed source-only next task, not an implementation or execution authorization. No product code or numerical geometry replay was run for this note.

## Why it is worth a finite review

A downward binary64 bound L on the L1 sum of one actual included native source vertex can screen other exact candidate folds without an ideal-sphere theorem. For each current vertex, retain the original upward bound U and the original fold whenever `U >= L`. Skip a fold only for strict `U < L`.

On a complete stable native traversal, let M be the actual exact maximum. Since the seed belongs to that traversal, M is at least the seed's exact L1 sum, which is at least L. Every point attaining M has U at least M, so its fold cannot be omitted by this new screen. This is a source-level mathematical argument about actual supplied values. It does not establish authority, operand/error equivalence, a cost saving or any product outcome.

The proposal can remove an entire six-charge original candidate fold for one added comparison and finite setup. That is structurally more promising than replacing each six-charge fold with another exact representation that still performs most constituent arithmetic.

## Necessary finite cost ledger

For each eligible completed direct scan, use:

`net = 6(B - K) - E - A - F`.

B counts actual baseline exact candidate events; K counts proposed exact candidate events; E counts new charged strict-screen comparisons, assuming exactly one unit for each; A counts all setup/admission/selection/copy work; F counts additional fallback/repeated work. Add any other new ordinal or per-vertex work explicitly.

This is not `5B - A`. The new exact incumbent can remain smaller because earlier candidates were skipped, causing the old upper screen to pass on more vertices. With a seed selected at ordinal 133 and a schedule that skips the earlier candidates, the proposed seam can be reached at every earlier point even though the baseline often screens them out. An upfront exact radius is not assumed.

Questions the next task must answer:

1. **Seed selection:** Is index 133 selected only for an admitted 482-point native mesh? What actual source selection, length/type checks and retained occurrences are introduced? Can this be done only after the existing extrema cache has missed or fallen back, with zero warm-hit overhead?
2. **Lower-bound construction:** Which native operations compute L? A schedule with three absolute values, two downward-rounded additions and necessary finite checks must charge all newly executed arithmetic/rounding/classification. Do not silently treat a broad setup label as permission to combine distinct required operations. Include any native reference or copied scalar occurrence.
3. **Baseline B:** Can the admitted domain establish a useful source-derived lower bound on original candidate events after the original upper screens and repeated-maximum witness? No event count has yet been measured or proved for an original recording.
4. **Proposed K and E:** Can the same domain bound the number of surviving folds and added comparisons, including changed-incumbent feedback? Neither number may be inferred by subtracting from 482 or by counting eight idealized maxima.
5. **Fallback funding:** What work is incurred when seed admission fails or live authority changes? Does the path resume without replay, or pay a full extra scan? Include failed/partial attempts and producer work with no benefiting consumer.
6. **Request aggregation:** Count actual cold direct attempts, warm hits, cache evictions and replay. A sphere occurrence or animation frame is not necessarily a fresh cold scan. Do not credit baseline reuse again.
7. **First exceptions:** Does every new operation occur under an authority/domain where it cannot replace an earlier original malformed-vertex, conversion, arithmetic, callback or resource failure? A net full-scan saving alone does not prove prefix behavior.

## Seed inclusion is a distinct authority obligation

`_vertex_extrema_uncached` traverses `zip(source_positions, stored_positions)`. It does not itself require equal sequence lengths before that traversal. The wrapper sends incompatible shapes to this direct path, and stored positions may be a mutable list. Thus a point selected from the full source tuple is not automatically in the actual processed zip prefix.

If a callback truncates the stored sequence before index 133 is reached, the seed may never be part of the processed scan. L then need not be a lower bound on that scan's maximum, and omitting earlier folds can change the result. The earlier caller length check alone does not establish stable future coverage. The candidate must establish seed inclusion in the actual traversal or disable/invalidate its shortcut with complete accounting and correct failure ordering.

Likewise, native float type and tuple length do not establish constructor identity or unchanged operations. A pre-read of an unvisited point must not execute custom methods or raise an exception that the original scan would encounter only after an earlier failure. All source-to-binary32 comparisons and error accumulation remain at their original point in source order.

## Comparison with dyadic replacement of each fold

A concrete native-integer alternative would normally require three float-to-ratio conversions, exponent/common-denominator selection, alignment shifts, exact integer additions, operand guards, final exact result construction and comparisons. Under a straightforward schedule, three conversions, two additions and one exact result construction already consume six operation units before shifts, selection and authority. This is only a schedule-specific warning, not a proof against every integer representation.

The lower-witness screen has a clearer potential source of operation savings: actual entire candidate folds can disappear. Its major remaining cost question is whether the saved folds exceed changed-incumbent comparisons plus complete setup/fallback work on a proved domain. A finite source-only review of that question is more promising than starting with a replacement arithmetic representation whose positive margin is unidentified.
