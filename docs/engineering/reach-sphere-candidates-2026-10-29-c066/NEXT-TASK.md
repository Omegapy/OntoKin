## Bounded question

Can one actual, retained source vertex supply a conservative lower bound on the final exact L1 radius that removes enough currently executed cold candidate folds to pay for its complete setup, selection, authority and fallback work?

The target remains `_vertex_extrema_uncached` in `ontokin/reach_conformance.py`. This is distinct from C066's ideal-lattice shortlist: it does not assume which sphere vertex maximizes the radius and it does not use a sphere or native-trigonometric error theorem.

For a selected actual source point `q`, derive a finite nonnegative float `L` satisfying `L <= abs(q.x)+abs(q.y)+abs(q.z)` exactly. Downward-rounded additions of the actual nonnegative coordinates are one candidate method; all subnormal, zero, overflow and changed-dispatch cases require a proof and explicit rejection behavior. Do not calculate an exact future Fraction sum ahead of earlier vertex validation.

At a point's original cold candidate seam, preserve its actual vertex tick, source-to-binary32 comparison, existing float upper bound `U`, repeated-maximum witness and error accumulation. A new **strict `U < L`** test may exclude its exact fold only when the bound and authority are valid. Otherwise retain the original exact fold. The seed is not substituted for the final returned radius.

## Mathematical premise to establish

If the seed belongs to the complete set actually traversed, its exact L1 sum is at most the true maximum `M`, so `L <= M`. Every true maximizing point has `U >= M >= L` and fails the strict exclusion test. At least one actual maximum therefore receives its original exact fold. Strict comparison protects equality; a `U <= L` replacement needs a different argument and is outside this nomination.

This is a conditional exclusion theorem, not an implemented algorithm. Prove the actual downward-bound operations and the seed's membership in the traversed set. The current helper uses `zip` without its own length check. An unequal or callback-shortened stored sequence could stop before a future seed, invalidating the argument. An earlier caller length check alone does not establish stable complete coverage across callbacks. Define a justified immutable/full-coverage domain or a funded continuation that cannot successfully return without validating seed inclusion.
