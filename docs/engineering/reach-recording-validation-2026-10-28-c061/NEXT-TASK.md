The descriptor must preserve the exact root, ordered joints, source path and offsets wherever the helper contract requires them.

These are ceilings on the whole measured phase bodies, not measured ancestry cost or removable work. Do not attribute the entire pool to `_ancestry`.

The observed returned paths overlap across nested carriers. This is a concrete repeated-walk hypothesis, not evidence that the required source authority or replacement cost is affordable.
