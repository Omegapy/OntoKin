## Mandatory qualification

Execute two fresh `.ok` builds for each A–F case, plus direct-Python builds at
E (362.5 mm) and F (372.5 mm). Reopen and resimulate all fourteen saved scenes
in fresh pinned Blender processes. Require source/geometry/native configuration,
continuous mapping, contact, twenty unrelated-pair separation, exact repetition
and route-equality checks. Every native carriage X sample and all 481 timestamps
must survive the existing recorded mapping. Both moving mass owners remain
1 kg. Removing the left shoe collider must restore the frozen pass-through
behavior. The mapping limit stays 0.2 mm; all other original tolerances remain.

Run the translated 42 lifecycle cases and the two explicit store-namespace
checks against a qualified A store. The controlled copying workers reuse its
retained native result; they are not new physics trials. Admission, cancellation,
descendant cleanup, rollback, output failure and accepted-state integrity remain
mandatory. Inspect actual raw/final GLBs with the pinned receiving applications
and visually review the imported final cases. Relevant legacy regression and
resulting-main checks are separate integration gates.

The qualification sources explicitly request 1,000,000 operations and 80,000 ms;
this decision authorizes those finite host ceilings for these named attempts.
Ordinary defaults remain 100,000 operations and 30,000 ms. Source/native byte,
object, sample, scheduled-substep and cancellation limits stay fixed. Commanded
substeps are the declared native work charge; internal Bullet iterations are
not telemetry. The exact 400-iteration guide setting and supervisor deadline
bound the additional native work.

The original v01 code, C191/C192 contracts, frozen gates and rejected results
remain unchanged. If any required C193 case fails, report the new backend as
unqualified and preserve the failure. Another change needs a new explicit
decision. The inherited torque timeout and full-project obligations remain
separate; this adoption does not pass them.
