# Localized assembly repair: protocol C253/0.1

## Question and admissible claim

Can the same agent change one named component, rebuild an editable delivery,
preserve unrelated objects and reject an invalid request under matched limits?
This is an end-to-end workflow comparison. OntoKin, Blender/Python and OpenUSD
use different authoring representations and possibly different exporters. A
future difference would therefore not isolate the effect of the language.

C253 freezes a **qualification protocol**, using the existing
[C210 proof](../semantic-export-c210/CONTRACT.md). It also states the
requirements for a later comparative study. The public fixture has already
appeared in demonstrations and source. Its answer is not a clean held-out
evaluation. No quantity of repeated runs turns it into multiple independent
assemblies. The independent assembly-family count is one.

The [C180 comparison principles](../semantic-physical-scope-c180/README.md)
and [C252 roadmap](../review-integration-c252/ROADMAP.md) govern this design.
Neither the old rotary/tray trial schedules nor C251 sampling is reopened.

## Frozen cases and source population

The [corpus manifest](corpus.json) identifies exact existing inputs at the
C252 main revision, their dependency closure and the expected change.

| Case | Initial state | Requested outcome | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | First rack's middle finger is 0.65 m long. | Change that occurrence to 0.75 m. | Valid repair; C210's original direction. |
| V2 | The same occurrence is 0.75 m long. | Restore it to 0.65 m. | Related reverse repair, not a second assembly. |
| N1 | The accepted V1 initial state. | Attempt the existing 0.75 kg length candidate. | Reject the incompatible unit and preserve accepted state. |

Each case starts from its own clean accepted source. N1 is not an extra repair
turn within V1. The exact before, after and rejected candidate files already
exist. No new `.ok` fixture or adjusted historical expectation is created.
The shared `example.hinges` 1.0.0 library remains byte-identical and pinned.

The candidate source may change only the selected occurrence initializer in
OntoKin. Equivalent direct routes may change the corresponding occurrence
parameter and its necessary generated output, while retaining their frozen
component definition and every unrelated meaning. They need not imitate
OntoKin syntax or its API. All routes receive the same requested dimensions,
units, identities, relationship meanings, animation requirements and tolerances.

The whole assembly remains in scope: 18 object identities; 34 sites; eight
hinges; eight follows; two actions; the original two-second motion. The selected
finger's declared length and its blade's dimensions, base site and dependent
world center may change. The 16 unrelated objects, including all 11 objects
of the second rack, keep their meaning and delivered geometry/motion. Source
positions and provenance hashes that must change after an accepted edit are
not mistaken for collateral semantic damage.

## Exposure and development separation

The selected source is public and known to protocol authors. The qualification
runner may use it for oracle controls. Future trial agents receive only the
starting state, task information and matched route reference packs. Expected
outputs, reverse-case solutions, historical repair transcripts, scorer source
and answer-bearing evidence stay outside their mounted workspaces. Separate
fresh contexts prevent V1's result from being handed to V2.

These controls limit direct answer leakage; they cannot undo public training
or author exposure. A later advantage study needs separately reserved, unseen
assembly families and an independently reviewed corpus before any measured
attempt. It must record exposure and development provenance. Selecting such
new assets is a separate scope, not an implicit addition to C253.

A route is eligible only after it passes the same finite starting-state and
output requirements. Missing installations, incompatible native packages and
unimplemented route scaffolding are **unavailable/unqualified**, not competitor
failures. A required semantic property that cannot be represented is
**unsupported**. Record the property and reason before attempts. Never remove
that property from the task merely to admit a route.

reported even if unavailable. Adding it later requires a new frozen route
roster and balanced order before any results are inspected. A two-route result
cannot be described as a three-route comparison. No live physics, perfect
surface normals or new destination-support claim is an endpoint here.

## Matched information, actions and outputs

Before admission, independently verify the baseline representations against
the same formulas and invariants. Provide the same neutral task sheet and
observable facts to each route. Route-specific syntax/API references may differ,
but neither pack may contain a worked solution for these cases. Freeze pack
hashes and separately account for the labor/tokens needed to create them.

Agents can inspect accepted source, invoke bounded validation and submit a
candidate through the same neutral trial harness. No human corrections,
internet access, cross-route results, scorer access or reuse of another
attempt's workspace is allowed. The harness provides the same revision check,
staging and accepted-directory promotion rules to every route. Native route
diagnostics are retained; common transaction protection is not credited as a
language-specific feature.

Every valid-case submission must provide editable source with its dependency
closure, a clean-rebuild command, its actual artifact bytes, stable identity
mapping and a provenance receipt produced by the external harness. The common
evaluator checks the full seven-time delivery. A small route reader may extract
measurements from a native artifact, but it must be independently qualified
and must inspect that artifact, not repeat a submitted JSON claim.

Different formats need not have identical mesh ordering or serialization.
Compare declared dimensions, relationships, transforms and unaffected content
using the [scoring rules](SCORING.md). Rebuild determinism is required within
the exact route/environment settings. Route-dependent loss is recorded, and
loss of a required property fails that task. The original strict Blender
normal failure remains failed; it is not relabeled by this finite comparison.

## Repetitions, cost and stopping

[RUN-PLAN.json](RUN-PLAN.json) freezes six repetitions per case per eligible
route, task pairing, balanced order, limits, analysis and execution blockers.
For two routes that is 36 attempts: 24 valid and 12 invalid. Repetitions are
conditional observations on three cases of one family. Report these counts
separately from the number of distinct assemblies.

Keep first submitted and final budget-limited outcomes separate. Preserve all
candidate, failed, missing and timed-out artifacts. Stop an attempt at its
first exhausted limit; never buy extra budget to rescue a route. An external
infrastructure incident retains its original attempt record. A rerun belongs
to a separately identified pair, without deleting the failed record.

Reusable environment/scaffold/oracle preparation and maintenance are setup
costs. Per-attempt model input/output/cache tokens, tool calls, elapsed seconds,
repair submissions and interventions are task costs. Report both; neither
amortize setup over an invented future workload nor treat a failed cheap
attempt as an efficiency win.

## Current completion and execution boundary

Protocol completion requires source/hash verification, consistent scoring and
analysis, independent review, visible baseline gaps and recoverable source.
It does not require launching any baseline. Missing model snapshot, route
qualification and clean evaluation families block trial admission explicitly.

The next authorized boundary is the [offline oracle and manifest
component](NEXT-TASK.md). It may establish that the proposed rules reject
known faults. It may not run models, launch native applications, implement a
general runner or select new evaluation assets. R07 and R08 retain their
pending states. Comparative advantage remains unproven.
