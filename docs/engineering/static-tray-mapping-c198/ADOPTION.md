# Finite static-tray mapping decision

**Decision:** `ADOPT_FOR_BOUNDED_IMPLEMENTATION`  
**Contract:** `OK-MAP-STATIC-TRAY-001/0.1.0`  
**Selected experimental destination:** `ontokin.static-tray/0.1.0`

This decision adopts the bounded mapping and its implementation obligations.
It takes effect for repository development when the exact frozen inputs receive
independent approval and are integrated under the current coordination policy.
The [result record](results.json) and [independent review](evidence/review/REVIEW.md)
identify those observed events separately. Adoption does not make the adapter
available, qualify a tray, change standard exports, or admit an author exercise.

## Why this increment fits the goal

The [C197 feasibility review](../equipment-tray-c197/FEASIBILITY.md) found existing
language support for typed dimensions, reusable definitions and independently
identified instances. It found no qualified shared construction path for the
equipment trays. This mapping supplies a finite implementation target for that
specific gap. The intended result lets both OntoKin and direct Python construct
the same family through one worker, so later comparisons can hold the backend
constant while testing localized source repair and reuse.

The mapping reuses the adopted core/construction compiler. It introduces no
language grammar, no new physical meaning and no general importer. Two trays,
each with a base, four walls and one payload measurement proxy, define the
complete supported family. The specification limits dimensions, identities,
source relationships, placements, materials and orientations. Unsupported
requests must fail before native construction.

## Adopted inputs and precedence

The [specification](SPECIFICATION.md) governs source admissibility, construction
records and delivery meaning. The [record schema](canonical-record.schema.json)
provides structural checks; the specification's semantic checks remain required.
The [validation plan](VALIDATION-PLAN.md) and [control inventory](CONTROLS.json)
define independent measurements, failure controls and future attempt limits.
The [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) orders the executable work.
[INPUT-FREEZE.json](INPUT-FREEZE.json) binds the exact normative bytes and their
baseline source evidence. A conflict between these inputs blocks implementation
until resolved through an identified amendment; it must not be silently settled
by changing the producer or expected results.

Known dependency identities are historical inspected pins until verified on the
execution host. Future adapter, worker, checker, fixture and launch-lock bytes
cannot be hashed before they exist. Their complete immutable implementation
closure and independent approval are mandatory before any native qualification
command. This explicit later gate is not a claim that those bytes are frozen now.

## Scope, authority and limits

The user has authorized routine technical development and repository integration.
This is a technical selection within that mandate. No new owner confirmation is
required for the next scoped implementation. Actual unsupported semantics,
unavailable dependencies or conflicting source evidence require a recorded
revision or narrowed result; they do not authorize a general backend expansion.

The current C198 decision runs **zero native exports, simulations, receiving jobs
and timed authors**. The future qualification plan uses its own finite, assigned
attempt ledger. A later tray admission remains separately frozen and capped at
four native exports total: one baseline and one clean regeneration per route,
including failures, without automatic retries. Author comparison follows only
after both routes qualify and the separate admission gates pass.

The shared C193 carriage adapter, earlier contracts, ordinary defaults of
100,000 operations / 30,000 ms, runtime, tests, fixtures, numerical gates and
hosted-workflow policy retain their bytes and meaning. The inherited legacy
driver and supplemental tail remain **FAILED**, with **4,239 distinct passing
methods and thirteen torque-read methods NOT RUN** after the original 30,000 ms
setup failure. Full regression, release, compatibility/external roles, general
contact, live destination physics, comparative advantage and the complete
[GOAL](../../../GOAL.md) remain open.
