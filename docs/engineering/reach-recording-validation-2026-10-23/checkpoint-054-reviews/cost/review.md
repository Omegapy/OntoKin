## 3. D36 is an explicit generic screen, not a universal minimum

The coordinator selected D36 for cost assessment as a conventional conservative current-function-state screen. For each of the following 36 named Python callable slots, a completed screen performs five distinct identity predicates against its authenticated expected state:

1. Current callable binding.
2. Current `__code__` object.
3. Current `__defaults__` reference.
4. Current `__kwdefaults__` reference.
5. Current `__closure__` reference.

The predicate tariff is one new shared operation each. Field reads, unwrapping property getters or static methods, container checks, closure-cell contents, keyword-default contents, globals/builtins, owner shadows and storage authority remain additional work. Identity of a mutable container or a closure tuple does not certify its contents. The trusted expected-state source and safe executable precharge are also open obligations. Five comparisons are a **partial cost**, not a complete safe gate.

The 19 arithmetic definitions were independently located by AST inspection. CPython 3.12.14 primary `Lib/fractions.py` confirms the twelve Fraction callables/getters, including the four separately generated forward arithmetic closures. For one completed D36 screen:

`D36_predicates = 36 * 5 = 180`.

The most favorable single realized publication opportunity therefore satisfies:

`local saving <= 163 - 180 - other_new_cost = -17 - other_new_cost`,

where `other_new_cost` is nonnegative in this local completed, stable, convergent comparison. This needs **one** complete D36 screen. It does not assume the entire screen repeats after the final publication tick. The publication tick is already deducted in the 163 and must not be charged twice.

An early rejecting D36 prefix can cost fewer than 180 predicates, but produces no saving through that rejected publication. Its reached work still counts. The 180 is not an exact gate total, not a measured count, and not an all-path statement about every rejecting prefix.

The inventory is deliberately not claimed complete. For example, failure paths can call `ReachExecutionWork.resource` and `ConstructionBudget.exceeded`; Fraction closures delegate to arithmetic helpers and comparison helpers; native owner storage and `_base_tip`/key construction require additional analysis. Omitting their costs makes the stated D36 bound optimistic, not safe for implementation.

There is a material design qualification. Eighteen of the nineteen listed arithmetic functions have no parameter defaults; `remember` has the keyword-only `admission=None`. An exact use-sensitive proof may avoid checking metadata that cannot affect the particular native call. Exact endpoint certification may also avoid authenticating unused work/check/observer fields of the already-finished child. The generic D36 rule chooses to inspect those function metadata references anyway. Accordingly, **180 rejects D36, not every semantically sufficient gate**. A different route must identify the actual reads and effects it preserves, rather than silently inheriting D36's cost or treating omitted obligations as free.

## 4. Literal T2 repair and remaining unknowns

A distinct literal linear repair, T2, rejects every first tag equal to `"pi"`, `"reference_pose"` or `"sincos"`, in addition to the tuple/nonempty/exact-string predicates. Six predicates per visited key give a complete-pass lower bound of `6m`. Two passes therefore cost at least:

`6*(m_before + m_after)`.

Only with stable occupancy is this `12m`. At stable occupancy 14 the key predicates alone cost at least 168, above 163. At occupancy 13 their 156 predicates leave at most seven operations before every other new cost. No actual setup occupancy is established here. Different branching or membership implementations have their own costs; this bound concerns the literal three-string-comparison screen.

T2 excludes the named cache-key equality witness. It does not establish safe target-map access, source owner/descriptor reads, bound checks, counter maps, dictionary watchers, safe precharge, absence of concurrent mutation or a passive lifetime through first use. Exact native-value equivalence, prospective domains and certificate provenance also remain required. A smaller key-only lower bound at small occupancy is not a positive complete gate or permission to select a collector.
