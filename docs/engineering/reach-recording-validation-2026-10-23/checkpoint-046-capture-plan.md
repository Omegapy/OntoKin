## Instrumentation boundary

The only replaced dispatch targets are `gltf.validate_export` and `reach_conformance._key_comparisons`. Each wrapper calls its original target exactly once with the same positional/keyword objects and owner identities. Both original targets are restored in `finally`.

Do not replace arithmetic, cache, guard, source, callback or observer methods. Do not install `sys.settrace`, `sys.setprofile`, monitoring hooks or a new work observer. Current native-field and product gates inspect trace/profile state and captured dispatch identity. A trace that leaves arithmetic methods unchanged can still alter the executed route.

Observation reads only admitted native fields and existing exception frames. It calls no pose calculation, source query, charged helper, cache lookup/promotion, domain gate or independent numerical oracle. Exact primitive snapshots use a fixed native codec; it explicitly covers Fraction integer fields, Interval endpoints, PoseEnclosure fields, SourceBound's 18 fields, relevant centered/source records, native tuple/list/dict containers and bounded native frozensets of strings. There is no generic `repr`, stringification or dynamic dataclass traversal of live product objects.

Unsupported shapes and diagnostic bounds produce observation errors. They must not replace the original product exception, produce an accepted artifact or silently omit an entry while claiming complete capture. The driver is not intended to establish neutrality for arbitrary hostile or reentrant objects; such observations are invalid for this study.

## Interpretation and stop conditions

Boundary snapshots establish boundary state and order. They do not directly observe every lookup, promotion, temporary insertion, publication or eviction inside a key. Distinguish a charged refusal before membership from a completed `None` lookup and from a completed hit. A current no-eviction or new-key inference requires its own closed source/authority proof, using the current facts rather than assuming C033 applies.

Do not select an implementation solely from a repeated seed or a nominal 60-charge helper difference. Require actual structural opportunity, exact operands and proof method, every added/removed charge, bounded storage/lifetime and independent numerical/authority review. Preserve the 64-update limit, direct source equation, stopping degree, remainder proof, guards, pending publication and all failures.

If the prepared driver cannot collect enough information neutrally, report the specific missing event and preparation blocker. A finer event-level mechanism is a separate task. It must not be replaced by tracing through gates that explicitly change behavior under tracing.
