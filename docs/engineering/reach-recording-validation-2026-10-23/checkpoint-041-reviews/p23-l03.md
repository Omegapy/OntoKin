## P23 constructor candidate and narrow disproof

`ReachSimulator._constant_pose` (`reach_runtime.py`, lines 534-540) looks up `("reach_source_constant_pose", inverse, value.position, value.rotation)`, builds the ordinary full pose on a miss, and publishes it under that key. The key has no recording namespace, phase, graph, or request-local recording-context field. The local helper at lines 1029-1032 still calls this full-pose helper when alternate recording arithmetic is absent.

`ReachPoseOps.constant_pose` calls `make` (`reach_pose.py`, lines 1189-1203). Its noninverse construction sequence is: current position admission; current rotation admission/cache; default velocity admission; default angular-velocity admission; stationarity check; stationary-pose cache lookup; full `PoseValue` construction on a miss; stationary-pose insertion. The surrounding source-constant cache lookup and insertion also remain.

Replacing only the constructor with `make_recording` would publish `RecordedTransform`, which lacks `exact_velocity`, `exact_omega`, and `position_error_m`, under the full-pose key. A subsequent or reentrant full-source cache hit could therefore return a value missing required fields. `compose_recording` accepting the shorter type does not repair this shared result-contract violation. A separate private leaf or namespace would be a different design with new cache/lifetime costs.

If the result type and both cache layers remain unchanged, the unconditional gross target is just two `pose_vector_domain` calls for the default zero derivatives plus one `pose_stationary_check`: **three charges per eligible construction**. Let `m` count calls that actually reach this constructor after the outer source-constant cache misses. With setup authority work `A`, per-call checks `h_i`, and additional lifetime/storage/fallback cost `L`, positive saving requires:

`H = A + sum(h_i) + L < 3*m`.

Neither this complete charged bound nor current P23's `m` is established. Outer cache hits offer none of those three charges. The original stationary-cache callbacks and errors cannot be omitted while claiming unchanged cache behavior.

The existing `_native_recording_rotation_domain`/`static_context` checks concern orientation sharing. They do not establish all constructor function-code/default/global/builtin identities, deep default-zero integrity, current work/counter authority, observer/trace/profile behavior, or the removed callback/exception sequence. They cannot be borrowed as a free general proof. The alternate `Binary64RecordingOps.recording_constant_pose` route already has its own `_direct_identity_source_leaf` shortcut (`reach_recording_fast.py`, lines 982-1022); that is not a new saving available to claim for the ordinary first/boundary graph.
