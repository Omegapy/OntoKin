## 2. Result of the investigation

The [derivation](checkpoint-034-offset-products.md),
[static charge ledger](checkpoint-034-cost-ledger.json) and
[independent reviews](checkpoint-034-reviews.json) separate three results.

**Exact arithmetic:** for the same native operands, reusing completed
`d*b` and `c*b` preserves the original combined expressions and final
128-bit-grid floors. Signed products, parent-position additions, radii and
pending source admission remain ordinary. Conservative prospective bounds
are **673 numerator bits / 645 denominator bits**, valid only within the
unchanged full 32,768-bit domain.

**Ordinary memoization:** a separate bundled receipt costs five selected
charges versus four originally; a source-known donor with insertion and
lookup costs four; two separate scalar receipts cost eight. These forms are
held for nonpositive savings before any extra admission work.

**Fused same-slot design:** retain the original coefficient insertion,
compute the donor's original products, then add one conditional immutable
replacement of that same coefficient slot. The selected recipient obtains
the products through its existing coefficient lookup. The conditional trace
is **four selected charges to three**, a local saving of one operation.
This is not a measured optimization or complete-request prediction.

The design must select two **nonstationary** sibling projections. Otherwise
a recipient's stationary-pose cache may bypass its offset and erase the
saving. Select at most **one pair per coefficient-source segment**; disjoint
endpoints alone allow interleaving that overwrites an earlier donor receipt.
Nonselected coefficient hits must preserve the expanded payload.

A fresh strong evaluation token, exact key-clock/source/phase identities,
native dispatch latched at every remaining callback and checked at each
skip, fixed-size sealed fields, detached operands and expected-slot identity
after callbacks are mandatory. An evicted or replaced slot must not be
resurrected. The work cache and independent arithmetic cache are separate;
the checkpoint033 work-cache occupancy does not establish the independent
cache's occupancy.
