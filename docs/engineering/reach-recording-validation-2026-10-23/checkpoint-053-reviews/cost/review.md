## 1. Frozen S1 schedule

S1 retains the original `_canonical_targets` common algebra, fresh children, precision schedule, atan calculations, target rounding, half-turn normalization, closed limits and original exceptions. Its new work begins only after all original target checks have succeeded, immediately before the original return.

1. Charge one added entry/precision discriminator. A later-precision success returns the original result after this one reached charge.
2. For first128 success, perform charged current owner/method/code/global/domain/observer/cache admission. There is no assumed free native receipt or zero-cost safe access.
3. Require exactly one matching completed child pi entry and native endpoint storage. Do not construct a missing pi or consult a parent numerical value as target authority.
4. Recursively inspect **all** resident parent keys for the schedule's passive safe component domain. Perform a charged, nonpromoting absence check. A present key falls back without overwrite or promotion.
5. Retain the child's existing `Interval` by alias and invoke native parent `remember`. Charge all new retained/copy/output occurrences separately where required; aliasing is not a free proof of safe lifetime.
6. After the original publication tick and any cache-domain synchronization, run the precharged, callback-free final admission before insertion. It must recheck the chosen identities, value, matching domain and genuine absence. Propagate original callback/resource exceptions unchanged. Rejection keeps all reached charges.

This fixes the order and the intended admission domain. It is a source schedule, not an implemented complete predicate. Its safe precharge mechanism, complete per-field gate cost, closure through the earlier child computation and all changed later work have not been proved. The favorable cost bounds below grant these unknown costs their most optimistic nonnegative value; they do not assert that value is zero.

An original `_targets` key hit never enters `_canonical_targets`. Failed first128 attempts and original failures do not reach this new gate. If an original later precision eventually succeeds, S1 reaches only the added entry/discriminator and returns without publication.

## 2. What can actually be saved

The unchanged native128 `pi()` performs one lookup, then the following cold work:

An eligible native pi hit costs one. C052's source ledger and the unchanged arithmetic support the difference `165 - 1 = 164`. S1 does not eliminate any canonical-child work. It can eliminate this difference only at a later parent call that would otherwise be cold.

The candidate's early `parent.remember` adds one stable-domain tick before that later hit. Therefore the most favorable matched corridor, with one publication and one realized eliminated miss, has

```text
165 - (1 early publication + 1 later hit) = 163
```

before the entry discriminator, authority checks, child lookup, key scan, absence check, retained occurrence, final admission and any other new work. Constructor avoidance supplies no saving: S1 keeps the original child, whose constructor has no tick in any case.

The reader cache `arithmetic.cache` and production work cache `work._cache` are different mechanisms. Native reader `remember` already covers its ordinary bounded membership/insertion/eviction bookkeeping with one tick. Do not import the production cache's separate promotion/eviction charges into this count. Additional admission, recursive scans and copies are still new charged work under budget-counting section 3.

## 3. Conditional ordered-cache convergence

Early insertion does not necessarily create an unbounded later-cache penalty. A useful narrow source theorem exists.

Assume an authentic child pi with exactly the original parent128 endpoints; unchanged native methods, fields and operand domain; passive keys; a fully closed callback/observer graph; no escaping alias or identity-sensitive observer; and effect-free native `_event` target-map/owner storage. Also assume the original `_base_tip` lookup and its first nonzero `sincos` lookup both miss, and parent pi is genuinely absent.

After canonical success, `_event` stores the target and calls `_base_tip`. The latter performs its own lookup, then the first sine/cosine lookup. Under these premises the two misses do not promote or insert anything. No native parent-cache mutation occurs between early pi insertion and the first parent pi use.

With fewer than 256 entries, both schedules ultimately append pi after the same ordered keys. At capacity, S1 evicts the same oldest key early that the original later pi insertion would evict. The intervening misses do not need that key. S1's pi hit promotes the already-last pi entry; the original cold call inserts pi last. At this point their **ordered keys and numerical contents** agree.

This is not object-identity equality. S1 retains the child's `Interval` and `Fraction` objects, while the original parent constructs new objects. Extensional native consumers must be proved insensitive to that difference. Under this stronger closed corridor, remaining native numerical work and cache order can agree and the signed remaining-work difference is zero. This prevents an unsupported claim that every early insertion must damage later cache behavior.

Outside the corridor, the conclusion does not follow. An early eviction can turn an original base-tip or sine/cosine hit into a miss. A zero first argument can defer pi use. Another cache hit can promote an entry. Domain resets, changed operands, later callbacks, custom target-map keys and owner descriptors can change the route. A nonfull cache at publication alone does not prove subsequent neutrality.

Original L03's first authored unit-length target `(0,1,0)`, positive elbow at time zero, supplies the conditional source opportunity identified in C052. It does not measure the actually successful precision, S1 eligibility, the gate's cost, or completed-request savings.

## 4. The chosen full scan has an occupancy barrier

Let `m` be the number of resident parent entries when S1 reaches its full scan. Even granting only one charged inspection per entry, that scan costs at least `m`. S1's recursive component/type/field checks can cost more. For one realized eliminated cold miss and one stable publication,

```text
local saving <= 163 - m - A_other,
A_other >= 0.
```

Thus at 256 entries the ceiling is **-93 before other costs**, and at `m >= 163` this chosen route has no positive immediate room. This is a sufficient, deliberately optimistic threshold, not a sharp one: the explicit entry charge already makes `A_other >= 1`. Actual admission occupancy is unknown. The bound does not count every recursive component as free.

This is a bound on S1's full-scan schedule, not every possible safe absence mechanism. The authority review notes a narrower possible key proof: an exact nonempty tuple with an exact string first component unequal to `"pi"` can be rejected by tuple comparison before examining its tail. Such a different schedule could avoid recursive inspection of unrelated key tails, but still needs a complete, charged proof for every resident key and mutation boundary. It has not been selected or validated here.

A strict no-eviction variant would reject at `m >= 256` before insertion. That avoids the immediate capacity eviction but does not establish numerical origin, later nonuse, callback safety or positive total saving. It cannot be described as an implemented repair. An early occupancy cutoff likewise changes eligibility and rejection costs rather than making existing scans free.

## 6. Complete accounting, including unfavorable paths

For matched **completed** requests with an authentic selected design, use

```text
saving = 164*H - Q - A_nonpublication - Delta_remaining.
```

`H` counts actual matched native128 parent cold misses changed into eligible hits. `Q` counts every reached added native `remember` charge, including an attempt whose final admission rejects. `A_nonpublication` includes all other new startup, discriminator, type/field/key/origin/limit checks, retention/copy/output work, rechecks, synchronization and cleanup. `Delta_remaining` is signed candidate-minus-original work outside the matched cold-versus-hit terms and the already charged new work. Do not count a reset or later miss twice.

Successful insertions and realized savings are different counts. A publication can be followed by a cached base tip, no pi consumer, early eviction, reset or interruption. Therefore neither `Q=H` nor `H=number_of_targets` is generally valid. The special `Q=H=1`, stable-domain, convergent corridor gives `163-A_nonpublication`. No request-specific value of any term has been measured.

| Reached path | Saving opportunity and required accounting |
| --- | --- |
| Original `_targets` hit | Canonical helper and S1 are bypassed; no local gate cost or publication. Any separately introduced startup work would still count. |
| Original common algebra, constructor, target or closed-limit failure | No post-success gate; preserve the original error and completed prefix. |
| First128 `_Uncertain`, then later original failure | No successful gate or publication. Preserve original refinement and failure. |
| Later-precision success | Exactly one added entry/discriminator charge; no publication, so zero matched saving and one reached operation of overhead. |
| First128 success, empty child | No pi construction for publication; charge all reached admission/lookup/rejection work. |
| First128 success, present parent key | Nonpromoting rejection; no overwrite and zero cold-miss saving. Charge the reached full scan and absence check. |
| Authentic child, absent parent, successful cold-consumer corridor | `H=Q=1`; at most 163 before all other costs; conditional remaining-work difference zero only under section 3's premises. |
| Successful insertion, no later matching miss removed | `Q>=1`, `H=0` for that insertion; account for changed order, retention and all later work. |
| Full parent | All scanned keys and components count; insertion can evict an unrelated key. The favorable convergent ceiling is -93 before other work. |
| Final admission rejects after `remember` tick | `Q` still increases. No successful insertion is credited. A preceding domain synchronization may already have changed cache state and must be counted. |
| Domain changes or unsupported current state | Charge every reached check/reset/rejection. A child completed under an earlier domain does not establish that the original later cold construction satisfies current prospective limits. |
| Malformed/custom owner, key, value, method or callback graph | No native cost formula is applicable without admission; avoid invoking effectful fields during the optional screen. Charge actual safe rejection work. |
| Callback exception, cancellation, operation/time/operand refusal | Retain the exact attempted/completed event prefix and original exception identity. The completed-request identity does not certify interrupted prefixes or unvisited tails. |

For an interrupted `remember`, a reached charge attempt and a counter increment can differ because callbacks and resource checks precede the increment. Record both; do not force the completed scalar formula onto a failed prefix. No subtracting 164 or 163 from a reported failure at 100001 establishes completion.

Replaying omitted callback counts consumes the corresponding saving and still does not repair semantic placement: S1's extra early publication callback occurs before the original cold pi boundaries. Complete callback eligibility and original prospective limits require source proofs, not count matching.

## 7. Decision and limits

S1 is rejected on origin behavior before selecting a collector or implementation. Independently, its complete gate cost is not closed, and its chosen full scan cannot save operations on the favorable matched corridor at occupancies of 163 or more. The review preserves the legitimate narrower native convergence theorem and the conditional 163-operation room before new checks; it does not assert universal impossibility for child-to-parent reuse.

The next bounded design review must specify a provenance mechanism before child computation or an independent exact numerical identity proof, and include safe current consumer/key/callback/domain closure with an explicit charged schedule. A mere post-success structural screen or more observations of target incidence is insufficient.

The nominated C054 parent-key subproblem has a concrete cheaper alternative to S1's recursive scan: require `m < 256`, and every resident key to be an exact nonempty tuple whose first component is an exact string unequal to `"pi"`. Any pi-tagged key rejects, even if its precision or domain differs. This can avoid inspecting unrelated hostile tails and avoids capacity eviction on the admitted route. Under the stated predicate-by-predicate tariff, its literal four tests per entry alone cost at least `4m`, before key/tag reads, other visits, all current authority and final rechecks. For two complete passes these tests cost at least `4*(m_before + m_after)`. The shorthand `8m` applies only when both passes scan the same stable occupancy `m`. These are bounds on that specified scan, not on every conceivable implementation. Low occupancy leaves a question to answer; it does not establish origin or positive full saving. C054 must not treat this key-only improvement as a completed repair of S1.
