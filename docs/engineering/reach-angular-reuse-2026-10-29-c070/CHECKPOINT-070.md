## Exact source and numerical dependency

In [reach_export.py](../../../ontokin/reach_export.py), `_certificate_quantities` first adds positional source error to `ep`. Then it adjusts quaternion error, constructs a chord bound and constructs an arc bound. The angular assignments contain one, four and four `_exact` calls. Their passive native numerical dependency is `(bound.angular_speed, width, incoming_eq, nsr)` plus the identified formula; their results are adjusted quaternion error and arc.

The complete `certificate_group` cache key includes full motion/enclosing bounds, geometry, positional error, shift and magnitude. Distinct full keys can in principle share the four angular values. Existing full-group hits already avoid the calculation and contribute no additional angular opportunity. The [event map](EVENT-MAP.md) preserves the preceding positional addition, all three range checks, each actual operand/prospective/result guard and current first-error/callback behavior.

The four numbers alone do not authorize a generic hit. An eager key can read `bound.angular_speed` before the original adjusted-error range failure. A hit can skip observable custom charges/guards. Existing operand-domain reset and insertion-generation protections remain useful and are explicitly recognized. They do not resolve every new authority obligation. Replaying all nine original metered seams would leave no angular operation saving before the new cache costs. An independently authenticated passive native schedule remains a distinct unresolved possibility.

## Complete-cost conditions

For `m` genuine eligible occurrences among existing full-group misses, with one necessary cold calculation and `m-1` resident angular hits, gross avoided arithmetic is at most `9*(m-1)`. Ordinary stable cache work already adds `m` lookups, one insertion and `m-1` promotions, totaling `2*m`. The remainder before admission, guards, eviction, refusal and collateral effects is at most `7*m-9`. For one occurrence it is negative. Distinct tuples or repeated evictions reduce the benefit further.

The [funding record](funding.json) uses a complete-request equation with explicit lookup, promotion, insertion, eviction, reset, admission, retained-guard and repeated/refused work, plus a signed difference for other work. That last term includes changed whole-group hits, displaced writer or reader entries and altered subsequent work. It can have either sign and has no established value. No new cache, larger limit or free unknown work is assumed.

Actual input equality is only one missing observation. No finite incidence measurement alone would discharge the authority, lifetime, accounting or displacement obligations. The prerequisite for neutral instrumentation is therefore unmet. C070 does not run requests merely to find out whether an arbitrary cache might help.

The formula starts from the 100,000 admitted shared operations, subtracts the overgenerous angular ceiling, and adds only two distinct mandatory tail categories. `_key_comparisons` charges `independent_key_pose` once per carrier at each wholly unvisited key. `_CertificateBatch.add` charges `independent_certificate_member` for every remaining row. These do not count the interrupted key's remaining computation, endpoint decoding, interval arithmetic, batch flush or any additional work. They are lower bounds on required future work, not measured or fully reconstructed tail costs.

Thus this isolated direct saving is insufficient for these two preserved requests under the matched-path assumptions. Changed cache order, another algorithm, partial benefit and combinations of changes remain outside that rejection. No C070 inference about whether P16 or P23 would be rescued is made.
