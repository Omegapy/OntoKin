# C252 decision: require comparative evidence before expansion

**Decision date:** November 16, 2026, America/Denver. **Authority:** the owner's
explicit instruction to integrate the supplied review instead of implementing
the C251 handoff task. [Scope #233](https://github.com/Omegapy/OntoKin/issues/233)
and [current status](../../../STATUS.md) record actual publication and checks.

## Direction and priority amendment

Retain [C210's semantic-authoring and checked-transport boundary](../semantic-export-c210/DECISION.md).
Accepted `.ok` source describes supported intent: named parts, dimensions and
units, relationships, physical declarations and source identity. Engines and
specialist tools supply rendering, general simulation and gameplay. Existing
normalized records, revision-safe edits, diagnostics and delivered-artifact
checks remain the implementation to reuse.

The next investment question is whether that source representation helps an
agent complete constrained object-editing tasks more reliably or with less
effort than matched direct-tool workflows. Structured source and a high test
count do not establish that benefit. Comparative advantage remains **unproven**.

C252 explicitly changes sequencing in C210 and the
[C221 recommendation tracker](../review-integration-c221/RECOMMENDATIONS.json):

- Select a localized-assembly-repair benchmark protocol before further R07
  source expansion. This is an early bounded preparation step toward R08.
- R08's localized-authoring component no longer waits for completion of the
  second receiver. Cross-target-reuse claims still require actual evidence from
  each target; a benchmark without that evidence cannot make those claims.
- Preserve R00, R01 and R02's completed scopes. R03–R06 remain pending; README
  navigation alone does not complete their examples, diagnostics, profile
  consolidation or installed-first-use criteria. R07 remains pending. R08
  remains pending until its actual applicable comparison evidence exists.
- The tracker's historical `immediateTask: R07`, its R07-first ordering and its
  R08-after-second-target dependency are superseded only to this stated extent.
  All nine recommendation records and 41 criteria remain preserved. The other
  criteria are not waived or marked complete.
- Defer C251's proposed seven-time, 126-pose native sampling component. Its
  source, reviews, held PR chain and exact next-task instructions remain
  recoverable. A later scope may resume it only after reconciling ownership and
  selecting a concrete dependency or separately justified receiver objective.

Broad language expansion and additional production adapters remain deferred.
An implementation may be selected later only to resolve a demonstrated task
failure or a necessary, bounded experiment dependency. A failed benchmark is
not permission to add speculative features until something passes.

The review's ordering is therefore resolved as **protocol → bounded pilot →
evidence-led receiver/OpenUSD work → generator complementarity → research
claim**, with separately justified dependency work allowed. These are gates,
not simultaneous implementation assignments or calendar promises.

## Preserved boundaries

No C252 change introduces syntax, profiles, adapters, generators, model training
or runtime behavior. The language specifications, source budgets, numerical
tolerances, closed native schedules and historical failures remain authoritative
within their original scopes. Existing release and acceptance requirements are
not replaced by the proposed benchmark thresholds.

An accepted `.ok` revision remains distinct from a candidate, evaluated state,
exported artifact and destination observation. A source/provenance label is not
proof that a receiving tool preserved a field. Unsupported and unobserved
behavior must remain visible. The next step is the separately bounded
[protocol task](NEXT-TASK.md), and C252 closes after verified saved handoff.
