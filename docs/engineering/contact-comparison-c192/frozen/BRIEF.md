# C192 public paired localized edit and reuse

This brief is fixed before participant dispatch. This is a public exercise in a
shared environment. Neither files nor the evaluator are claimed hidden.

## Shared design and task

The starter is the seven-part two-carriage gauge at a 380 mm left-stop near face.
Both routes use the unchanged qualified C191 native constructor, Blender 4.5.3
binary, guides, compound bodies, constant-feed motors, capture and recorded-GLB
binding. The direct Python starter supplies editable design data to that same
constructor without parsing .ok. That preparation is charged to reusable setup.

First edit: move only the left stop near face to **362.5 mm**. Keep its dimensions,
identity and collider relationship. Preserve the other six parts, their colors,
body/collider identities, following, drives and physics settings. The expected
left travel is 182.5 mm and ideal contact is 1.460 s. The right carriage must
travel 300 mm with 15 mm final clearance. Submit editable source, native scene,
raw and final GLBs, native records and all build/inspection receipts.

After the coordinator independently scores the first submission, the same agent
receives reuse: start from its submitted editable design, retain that exact first
source, and create the **372.5 mm** stop variant. Its expected left travel is
192.5 mm and ideal contact is 1.540 s. Regenerate both first and reuse targets.
Retained source variants qualify literal-source reuse only, not general classes,
parameters, libraries or programmatic composition in this finite prototype.

## Identical allowances

- Exactly two fresh subagents, fork_turns="none", default model/settings, one per
  route. Separate writable directories and local branches. No extra delegation.
- Each phase allows 20 minutes of participant wall time and at most three formal
  submissions. Preserve every intermediate source and failed command/build.
  Up to six native build attempts per phase, including rehearsal and failed
  native builds. Source inspection and independent checker calls are unrestricted
  within the time limit. Stop at the limit and submit the state actually reached.
- Each native build retains the C191 request of 1,000,000 operations and 80,000 ms.
  The common supervisor and native process limits remain unchanged.
- Provider token usage, provider cost and hidden internal model turns are not
  exposed by this environment. They remain unavailable; no token cutoff is claimed.
- Participants use the same checker, native-inspection wrapper and export utilities.
  No trajectory editing, timed-stop substitution, checker/gate change or repair of
  an already submitted scene. No edits to shared implementation or frozen inputs.
- The coordinator scores submitted hashes before requesting any repair, then
  rebuilds successful sources in fresh processes and independently reopens every
  retained successful native scene. Rebuilds/inspection do not count as participant
  edits. Preserve raw and projected output, commands, actual times and failures.

## Frozen acceptance

All C191 numerical gates are unchanged. Initial geometry 0.2 mm; fixed-part or
attachment drift 0.1 mm; final travel and signed gap 0.5 mm; contact-onset band
entry within 0.005 s of ideal; requested feed and settled speed within 0.5 mm/s;
settled-speed checks start 25 ms after ideal contact; rebound 0.1 mm; positive
penetration 0.5 mm; raw/final mapping 0.2 mm. Preserve 481 common 240 Hz times and
every native carriage X sample. Certify all 20 unrelated pairs continuously.

Native configuration must retain 13 objects, seven visible solids, two 1 kg mass
owners, one solver authority per lane, ten substeps/frame and 100 solver iterations.
The scene must reopen and resimulate without reconstruction or design repair.
Khronos/Three.js readback and actual receiving-application playback inspection
apply to the submitted raw/final hashes. Clean rebuild outputs must reproduce
canonical data, native poses and raw/final GLBs; .blend byte identity is not required.

## Evidence and interpretation

Report first-pass outcome, failed/invalid attempts, formal repair turns, observed
wall time, coordinator interventions and source changes. Separate starter/harness
preparation and independent validation from participant effort. No human intervention
is requested during the paired task. Report any that occurs.

Both successes qualify this workflow only. A single pair cannot establish a language
advantage, statistical reliability, blind evaluation, cost superiority, a production
backend, general contact or completion of GOAL. Equal success warrants no broad
expansion; choose another discriminating task before a larger study.
