# C077: matched-topology polygon ring lofts

After C076 integration, resulting-main and final checks, portable delivery and explicit scope closure, reconcile fresh main, STATUS, branches, PRs, hosted workflow state and [Issue #26](https://github.com/Omegapy/OntoKin/issues/26) under [AGENTS](../../../AGENTS.md). Preserve the complete [GOAL](../../../GOAL.md).

The next selected bounded modeling increment is source-authored polygon ring lofts with explicit correspondence across ordered sections. This extends C076 outlines and through-holes into tapered housings, chamfered profiles and hollow ducts. It connects editable ring geometry with the existing section, material, placement and reusable-part conventions. Selecting the task adopts no new syntax or limits and starts no worker or schedule.

1. Publish a separate versioned source/Agent/destination decision before implementation. Define finite section, ring and vertex bounds; station order and separation; matched ring roles, keys and traversal; deterministic strip and cap triangles; outgoing-edge material ownership; source identities and explicit budget admission. Preserve earlier profiles and defaults. No automatic topology matching, resampling or ring creation/removal is implied.
2. Resolve the exact surface meaning. A noncoplanar edge quad split into fixed triangles does not have the same intermediate cross-section as a straight line between linearly interpolated edge endpoints. Prefer an explicitly authoritative, deterministic triangular surface, or restrict quads to coplanar inputs if exact linear polygon slices are promised. The decision must make this distinction before fixtures are frozen.
3. Freeze independent positive, boundary, invalid, composition and repair cases: constant sections reproducing an extrusion; solid and hollow frusta with analytic volumes; a three-section chamfer; concave boundaries; multiple holes; reordering; numeric/count boundaries; and an exact one-occurrence repair with unchanged sibling and pinned library. Include valid endpoint rings whose connecting strips cross, pinch or fold, plus binary32 delivery failures. Valid authored sections alone cannot establish valid geometry between them.
4. Implement actual source construction, bounded section/ring/triangle inspection, and the complete Agent repair/evaluation/export/retrieval workflow. Require independent closed-edge incidence, cap coverage, surface intersections, normals, materials, volume and recorded motion, actual native/browser playback and capture review, the applicable inherited tests and verified integration under [OK-ENG-009](../OK-ENG-009-development-integration-and-branch-lifecycle.md).

Blind cavities, branching ducts, arbitrary bevel offsets, Boolean operations, automatic mass/inertia, collision shapes and live destination physics remain excluded unless separately adopted and verified. Original28, complete reach, compatibility and external roles, historical LFS migration, C059's unestablished storage cause and all other GOAL domains remain open. An open-ended operation-saving investigation is not a prerequisite.
