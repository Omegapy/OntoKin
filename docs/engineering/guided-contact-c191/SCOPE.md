## Intended increment

Adopt and publish a bounded experimental source, backend and recorded-output
contract before implementing its semantics. The source must own the seven-part
assembly, stable body/collider identities, two 1 kg mass owners, four contact
boxes, source frames, feed speeds and allowed stop edit. A small adapter maps
these declarations to the measured C190 Blender 4.5.3/Bullet construction.
Existing source, Agent and GLB `.22` behavior and all historical fixtures remain
unchanged.

Qualification covers the three prescribed stop positions, a disabled-collider
causal control, stable identity under rename/reorder, rejected edits, budgets,
cancellation, malformed output and publication failure. The native solve,
saved-scene replay, raw/final GLBs, exact X/time preservation and every declared
projection bound need separate evidence. Expected results and limits are frozen
before candidate output. Complete release and comparative authoring advantage
are outside this increment.

The baseline replay independently reproduced all six C190 route outcomes,
45 trajectory controls, three swept controls, twelve format checks and eighteen
Three.js poses. The existing Python local driver is running on the unchanged
base in a separate verification workspace. Its eventual actual outcome must be
reported, including failures, timeouts and unexecuted obligations.
