# Independent C191 qualification

The expectations and fixtures in this directory were fixed before the reviewer
inspected any adapter implementation or candidate output. They qualify the
finite experimental guided-contact binding. A playback pass alone does not
establish native contact causality.

## Frozen inputs

`frozen/SEMANTIC-FREEZE.json` fixes the analytic A–D outcomes, numerical limits,
twenty unrelated visible-part pairs and original .22 preservation hashes.
`frozen/CHECKER-FREEZE.json` identifies the independent playback reader,
continuous slab certificate, analytic control generator and its qualification:
8 positive controls, 54 negative controls and 3 swept-slab controls.
`frozen/SOURCE-FREEZE.json` adds exact source fixtures, diagnostic codes, phases,
UTF-8 byte spans, source-derived canonical inputs, the resource limits and
lifecycle failure cases. It binds the author's exact adopted contract manifest.

The source corpus has A–D, a renamed/reordered identity control, an omitted-budget
default control, 26 invalid-source cases and 5 runtime source negatives.
`frozen/lifecycle-fixtures.json` fixes 19 further lifecycle and malformed-result
checks, two clean builds per formal case and fresh saved-scene replays.

The ideal left contact times are `(stop near face − 180 mm) / 125 mm/s`:
1.6, 1.4 and 1.5 seconds. Disabling only the left shoe collider must instead
produce 250 mm of left travel and a −50 mm signed gap. Every case retains 300 mm
of right travel and 15 mm right clearance.

## Checkers

`checker/measure_c191.py` certifies the seven solid surfaces, colors, 481 common
keys, attachment/fixed-part stability, speeds, contact timing, final travel and
gap, 0.5 mm maximum positive-case penetration, and continuous nonoverlap of all
twenty unrelated pairs. The 0.5 mm/s residual-speed gate starts 25 ms after ideal
contact. Rebound is limited to 0.1 mm. The inherited decoder and rectangular
surface/swept-slab code are copied without modifying their historical files.

`checker/audit_mapping.py` uses the frozen C190 rotation/translation bound and
adds byte-level restrictions on the final projection. It compares every native
carriage translation sample, every time accessor and every changed binary byte.
The only allowed final changes remove carriage rotation channels and replace
transverse translation values with their initial values. Native-sample and
continuous raw-playback vertex deviations must each remain within 0.2 mm.

`checker/inspect_native.py` runs in a fresh pinned Blender process after opening
the delivered `.blend`. It imports no adapter implementation, inspects all
stored bodies, constraints, drivers and identities, clears the point cache and
replays frame 0 through 480. It creates no scene objects and performs no repair.
Its trajectory is independently captured from evaluated native matrices.

`checker/run_source_conformance.py` exercises the public prototype CLI against
the frozen source corpus. It checks the complete diagnostic envelope and exact
spans, the ordinary budget defaults, and canonical bytes derived independently
from the C190 dimension table. Runtime/lifecycle probes are separate evidence.

## Evidence boundary

The numerical playback controls are analytic fixtures. They are not solver
records. Native finite-difference velocities and geometric gaps are derived
from captured poses. No Bullet contact manifold, impulse, direct velocity or
unrecorded native substep telemetry is claimed.

Contract approval, branch integration, native qualification, receiving-application
validation, a full release and comparative authoring benefit remain separate
claims. Keep every failed attempt and each subsequent correction identifiable.
