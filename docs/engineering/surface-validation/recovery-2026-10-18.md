## Defects and corrections

Validated section centers and material colors could retain caller-owned lists. A callback could change a center after validation, and a caller could alter a material already returned in a mesh. The corrected generator constructs fresh immutable snapshots from the numeric values it actually validated.

The original minimum-station check used a fixed `1e-12 m` allowance. C1 replaces that unexplained tolerance with an explicit bound derived from the representation precision of the two canonical binary64 coordinates. Equal stations still fail. The supplement documents the exact rational comparison and its limits; it does not claim that every stored geometric gap is at least one millimeter. The original acceptance manifest and its expected results were not relaxed.

The repair also prevents an invalid key diagnostic from invoking a caller's arbitrary string conversion, distinguishes a malformed palette from malformed sections, and observes cancellation during final material grouping. Counts and remaining-budget rejection are tested before a complete result can be returned.

## Timeout and concurrent-write recovery

The visible application timeout did not stop the earlier session from publishing subsequent commits. A conditional repair publication encountered an unknown GitHub error; a confirming read found that the branch had advanced. The older tree was not forced onto it. The recovery preserved the other session's work, waited for its explicit lease release, acquired a uniquely named lease conditionally, and applied the tested changes on a separate recovery branch.

AGENTS now requires brief factual progress updates during long interactive work, smaller verified commits, distinct worker identities, and remote-state inspection before retrying any unknown write. These changes improve visibility and recoverability. They do not establish the cause of the application timeout or guarantee an uninterrupted network stream.
